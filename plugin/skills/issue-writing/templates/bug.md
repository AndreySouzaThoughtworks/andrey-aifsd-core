# Bug template

*Guidance in italics. Delete guidance as the bug is filled. Delete optional sections with nothing to say.*

## Impact summary

*State who or what is affected, the consequence, frequency, and severity. Separate observed impact from possible impact. A Bug does not need an invented user-story narrative.*

## Expected Result

*State the behaviour that should occur under the same conditions. Cite the contract, prior behaviour, product decision, or invariant establishing that expectation when it is not self-evident.*

## Actual Result

*State the observed result precisely, including relevant status, state transition, or user-visible symptom. Do not put a suspected cause here.*

## Environment

*Name the affected environment, version and configuration. This maps to the board's dedicated Environment field, so keep it to what identifies where the defect was seen.*

## Context

*State the boundary: what the defect is independent of, what conditions correlate with it, and anything a reader needs to interpret the evidence. Never include credentials, personal data, customer identifiers, internal hostnames, or secrets.*
